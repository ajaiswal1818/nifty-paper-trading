Plus v3i took one standalone trade v3 could not (24200 PE Jul 21, −₹421; v3 was blocked by
`no_opposite`, already holding). Net: holding beat same-day exit by ≈₹16.8k realized this week.

**This is the exact opposite of the 2019-2023 reconstruction** (DECISIONS 07-23: v3i same-day
+29.3% vs v3 hold −21.4%, normal-VIX). The reconciling variable is **regime**: that backtest was
a mean-reverting normal-VIX sample; this week was a sustained trend (NIFTY −2.33%, 5 straight down
days). In a trend, overnight holds of puts keep compounding as the market keeps gapping down;
same-day exits repeatedly cut winners at the intraday recovery. See §3 — the signal audit shows
exactly this mechanism. **Caveat: n = 1 week, 2 resolved shared trades.** This is the first data
point of the A/B, not a verdict. Do not act on it.

## 5. Candidate strategies

**No new candidate drafted.** The one systematic pattern this week — "signal predicts the
overnight/gap move but not the intraday session; holding wins in a trend" — is precisely the
hypothesis the **existing three-way live A/B already spans**: v3 (hold), v3i (same-day exit), and
v3-strong (stronger entry + hold, added Jul 24). Drafting a fourth book now would fork the
hypothesis before the current A/B has more than one resolved week. A cleaner future idea seeded by
§3 — an explicit *gap-capture* book that enters into the close/overnight and exits at the next
open — is a **human redesign** (new entry/exit structure, not a tunable), logged here as a seed
only. Revisit once the A/B has ≥4–6 weeks separating hold from same-day exit across both a
trending and a mean-reverting stretch.
