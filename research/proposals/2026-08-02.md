## 6. Open process items (for the human — not strategy params)

1. **v3i-next50 is structurally untradeable at current settings — decide its fate.** Its
   first-ever signal (Jul 31, score +3 ≥ entry 2) was correctly blocked by the sizing rule:
   nearest monthly ATM ≈ 1,020 pts × 1.03 slippage × lot 25 ≈ ₹26,388 + fees > the 20% cap
   (₹20,000) of ₹1,00,000 capital. Minimum position size exceeds the cap on *any* normal-VIX
   day, so the book can never enter — it is active-in-name-only and produces no A/B data.
   Options (all human redesigns, none proposable by this run): raise `size_cap` for this book,
   allow debit spreads at normal VIX to cut entry cost, raise its virtual capital, or park it
   `paused` until decided. Until then its 0-trade record is a config artifact, not evidence
   about Next-50 edge.
2. **Entry-fill defect** (carried 3rd week): fills at GIFT-implied spot, not the real open
   (Jul 31: 24,388 modeled vs 24,361 actual). Standing proposal: record the decision pre-open,
   fill at the monitor's first ≥09:15 real quote.
3. **Component-vote capture gap** (new, cheap fix): full signal breakdowns are only persisted
   in trade-log reasons, so no-trade mornings (most mornings) contribute nothing to component
   hit-rate audits. Suggestion: extend RUNBOOK step 9 to write the 5 component values into
   `morning_score_today` alongside the composite. Zero effect on trading; triples component-
   audit data velocity.

   flat days leave no heartbeat in the repo. A daily one-line heartbeat commit (or a timestamp
